# Figures

This folder contains the main figures and representative screenshots generated during the development of this project. Most figures were produced directly from the MATLAB programs in the `matlab` folder and are referenced throughout the undergraduate thesis.

---

## Figure 1 — Geometry Visualization

Three-dimensional visualization of the floating torus at a prescribed tilt angle. The figure illustrates the water surface, submerged region, center of gravity (GC), center of buoyancy (BC), and their geometric relationship.

---

## Figure 2 — Sample of Animation 1

Representative screenshot generated from Animation_1.m, which implements the interactive ideal hydrostatic stability simulator. Users can modify the tilt angle, geometric parameters, and density ratio to investigate the hydrostatic restoring behavior of the floating torus.

---

## Figure 3 — Sample of Animation 2

Representative screenshot generated from Animation_2.m, which implements the generalized hydrostatic stability simulator with an adjustable center of gravity. The model illustrates how internal mass distribution influences stable and unstable equilibrium configurations.

---

## Figure 4 — Hydrostatic Restoring Moment

Numerically computed hydrostatic restoring moment as a function of tilt angle.

---

## Figure 5 — Righting Arm

Numerically computed righting arm (GZ) as a function of tilt angle.

---

## Figure 6 — Hydrostatic Restoring Moment Near Zero Tilt

Enlarged view of the restoring moment near the equilibrium position, illustrating the approximately linear restoring behavior for small angular displacements.
